WM3020 - CONSERVATIVE 200 MM Y TRAVEL ROSE VARIANT

starburst.stl is a complete design with the original 225 mm diameter,
9 mm thick circular base and a reduced 190 mm diameter pleated relief.
The base represents the existing timber that fits the frame; it is wider
than a 200 mm Y range. The complete STL bounding box therefore still has
225 mm X/Y dimensions. It is not a claim that every part of that base can
be machined from one position.

The 190 mm pleated area keeps 29 peaks and 29 troughs (58 frame positions),
70 degree included folds and 35 degree inward edge lean. The flat border
is now 17.5 mm wide. The raised height becomes about 14.0 mm above the
9 mm base as a consequence of preserving the fold angle at smaller size.

Use the Y200 stretched-spiral facing file in ../facing for the flat timber.
Face once, rotate the stock 90 degrees, and run at the SAME work Z height
to complete the flat face. Set centre and fixtures to allow the specified
254 mm X by 196 mm Y travel. No controller settings were changed.

Later relief CAM must limit its machining area to the 190 mm relief and
appropriate cutter clearance. For example an outside profile of that
190 mm circle with a 5 mm cutter needs 195 mm centre travel. Verify the
actual generated toolpath bounds; do not mill a 225 mm outside-base
contour in one position on a 200 mm Y machine. Leave the existing round
base perimeter, or machine it separately with an indexed fixture.

Regenerate this variant with the parent generator:
make_starburst.py --diameter 190 --output wm3020-Y200 --preview
The original 210 mm pleated-area STL remains in the parent folder.
